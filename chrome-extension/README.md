# STANLEY BUSINESS MANAGER — Google Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

## Features
- **Toolbar Popup & Chrome Side Panel**: View live income, expenditures, net profit, outstanding balances, and recent receipts from any Chrome tab.
- **Quick Sale & Quick Expense Entry**: Record sales (with catalog auto-pricing) and expenses directly from the extension.
- **Offline Queue**: Entries recorded offline are saved in `chrome.storage.local` and synced automatically by the background service worker.
- **One-Click Full Workspace**: Open the full application in a browser tab for PDF receipts, WhatsApp sharing, analytics, and backups.

## How to Install in Google Chrome
1. Unzip `stanley-business-manager-chrome-extension.zip` into a permanent folder on your computer.
2. Open Google Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the extracted `chrome-extension` folder.
5. Click the **Extensions (puzzle)** icon in Chrome's toolbar and **Pin** **STANLEY BUSINESS MANAGER**.
